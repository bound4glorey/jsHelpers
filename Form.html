FormValidator Test Harness

1. Dynamic form (built via FormBuilder)

Fields here are built with FormBuilder.createForm(), a form with its own field list, and its createFormLine(). Trigger errors by leaving required fields blank, entering an out-of-range age or a future birthday, a malformed email, too short a name, and so on. Errors are also listed in an alert.

2. Static form (hand-written markup)

This markup was not built by FormBuilder — it's plain HTML, wrapped in FormElements and registered with its own FormValidator. Errors are written into the error containers you supply; anything you don't supply is left alone.
